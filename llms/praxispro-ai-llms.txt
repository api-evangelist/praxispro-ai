## What PraxisPro is

PraxisPro sells AI-driven sales-readiness software to pharmaceutical and medical-device
commercial teams. The platform is organized into four named modules:

- **PraxisPlay** — AI roleplay simulations against realistic healthcare-provider agents.
- **PraxisCertify** — certification tracking and knowledge-gap identification.
- **PraxisCoach** — performance coaching and structured feedback.
- **PraxisIntelligence** — analytics and insight reporting for sales management.

Users are sales representatives, trainers, managers, medical science liaisons, and compliance
and marketing teams. The company is backed by Techstars and raised a $6M seed round.
